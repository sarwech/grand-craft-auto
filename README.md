# Grand Craft Auto

A tiny papercraft crime caper you play in the browser: fly in over the coast, take a job from Marisol, borrow a convertible and drive to the marina.

**Play it:** https://sarwech.github.io/grand-craft-auto/

Inspired by @xikhar's papercraft skill.

## Stack

- [three.js](https://threejs.org) and xikhar's papercraft kit for the world. Everything is drawn in code, with no build step.
- [VEED Fabric 2](https://www.veed.io) for the talking heads: each character's face is a Fabric 2 clip made from one drawing and one voice line.
- ElevenLabs Eleven v4 for the voices.
- Procedural sound with the Web Audio API.

## Controls

- Walk with `W A S D` or the arrow keys, or click the ground.
- People talk when you get close. Pick a reply with a click, or `1` / `2`.
- Press `E` next to the teal convertible to get in. Drive with the same keys. Stop and press `E` to get out.
- `M` mutes the sound effects.
