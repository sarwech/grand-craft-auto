# Grand Craft Auto

A tiny papercraft crime caper you play in the browser: fly in over the coast, take a job from Marisol, borrow a convertible and drive to the marina.

**Play it:** https://sarwech.github.io/grand-craft-auto/

Inspired by @xikhar's papercraft skill.

## Stack

- [three.js](https://threejs.org) and xikhar's papercraft kit for the world. Everything is drawn in code, with no build step.
- [VEED Fabric 2](https://www.veed.io) for the talking heads: each character's face is a Fabric 2 clip made from one drawing and one voice line.
- ElevenLabs Eleven v4 for the voices.
- Procedural sound with the Web Audio API.

## Controls

- Walk with `W A S D` or the arrow keys, or click the ground.
- People talk when you get close. Pick a reply with a click, or `1` / `2`. `Esc` leaves a conversation.
- `E` or `Enter` uses whatever is next to you: talk, buy from the shop counter, hand over the box, get in a parked car, or take a stopped one from its driver.
- Drive with the same keys. Stop and press `E` to get out. `R` changes the radio station and `H` honks.
- Buy the flag gun in the corner shop. `F` or the right mouse button draws and holsters it, the pointer aims, and a left click or `Space` fires the flag.
- Stars mean the paper police are after you. Keep moving, or hide in the shop or on a pier until they fold away.
- `M` mutes the sound.
